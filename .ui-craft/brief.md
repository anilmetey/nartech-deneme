### 6. Learned constraints (optional, append-only)

- **2026-11-11** — Product detail pages should place product imagery in a contained, responsive media panel beside the localized title and description on desktop; keep technical specifications below the intro at full content width, and stack the image before the copy on mobile. *Why:* this keeps the product image visually balanced with its explanation instead of letting it dominate the page or push the useful content too far down.
